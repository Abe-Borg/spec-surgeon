## Your own version of Claude's drafted wording

- When Claude offers wording for you to pick, **Edit wording** opens it in a box already filled in. **Use this
  wording** picks the choice with your version, and your version is what the documents may carry; the draft you
  replaced is never approved. The edit must be one line of visible characters, the rule every offered wording passes.
